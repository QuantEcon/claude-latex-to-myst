# Kitchen Sink Chapter {#ch:kitchen}

A small integration test combining several transform families.

## Section One {#sec:one}

The Bellman equation is

$$\begin{equation}
V(x) = \max_a \{ r(x,a) + \beta \mathbb{E} V(x') \}.
\label{eq:bellman}
\end{equation}$$

We use \[\[CITEP:bellman1957\]\] as the foundational reference. The contraction property is established in [\[thm:contract\]](#thm:contract){reference-type="ref" reference="thm:contract"}.

## Section Two {#sec:two}

::: theorem
[]{#thm:contract label="thm:contract"}

The Bellman operator is a contraction.
:::
