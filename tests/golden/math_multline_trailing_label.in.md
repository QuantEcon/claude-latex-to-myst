# Multline Derivation

The first-order condition expands across two lines:

$$\begin{multline}
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial k_{t+1}^j}
= \beta^t U'(c_t^j)\\
+ \beta^{t+1} \mathbb{E}_t [U'(c_{t+1}^j) R_{t+1}] = 0.
\label{eq:foc_k}
\end{multline}$$
