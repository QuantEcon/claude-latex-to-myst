---

We impose the following conditions on the model primitives.

(a) the reward function $r$ is bounded and continuous

(b) the discount factor satisfies $\beta \in (0, 1)$

(c) the transition kernel $P$ is Feller

(d) the constraint correspondence is compact-valued

Condition (a) guarantees the objective is well defined. Conditions (b) and (c) together deliver a contraction. Condition (d) ensures a maximiser exists.
