We impose the following conditions on the model primitives.

\begin{itemize}
    \item[(a)] the reward function $r$ is bounded and continuous
    \item[(b)] the discount factor satisfies $\beta \in (0, 1)$
    \item[(c)] the transition kernel $P$ is Feller
    \item[(d)] the constraint correspondence is compact-valued
\end{itemize}

Condition (a) guarantees the objective is well defined. Conditions (b)
and (c) together deliver a contraction. Condition (d) ensures a maximiser
exists.
