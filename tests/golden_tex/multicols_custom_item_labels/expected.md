# Norms

Recall the norm properties:

(a) $\| u \| \geq 0$

(b) $\| u \| = 0 \iff u = 0$

(c) $\| \alpha u \| = |\alpha| \| u \|$ and

(d) $\| u + v \| \leq \| u \| + \| v \|$
